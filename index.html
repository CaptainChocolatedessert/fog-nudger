Fog Nudger

The map already shows where the rooms are. Stop drawing them again.

An Owlbear Rodeo extension that traces a map image into the fog regions you reveal room by room — then lets you correct what it got wrong. A proposal, not a result: automatic extraction cannot tell a doorway from a gap in the linework, and a merged room is a bug nobody sees until mid-session.

Works on its own. With Dynamic Fog installed, the same shapes become line-of-sight walls at no extra cost.

Pre-release — nothing to install yet.
