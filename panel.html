Connecting…

Everything that decides what gets traced is there: the map, the ink, the walls and the regions. It has to be its own surface because the answers are per-pixel verdicts, which only a picture can carry — and because the controls belong beside the thing they change rather than in a panel the picture would cover.

Closing the workspace puts the result on the fog layer, replacing whatever it put there before; there is a button inside for doing that without closing. There is no staging step any more. What the workspace shows is what the scene holds, so a hand edit to one of our fog items does not survive the next write — edit in the workspace instead.

Our fog

Takes out everything this extension put in the scene and leaves anything you drew by hand alone. Only needed to stop using the extension on a map; an ordinary re-run replaces its own output.

Diagnostics

Trace, emit nothing runs the whole chain and writes the numbers to the log — coverage, region counts, and the area check, which is two independent computations of the same figure agreeing. The workspace draws the same partition; this is the only thing that reports it in numbers. What is here? is no longer a button: click the map in the workspace.

Workspace probe puts up an opaque full-screen sheet that keeps its own input, which is what the workspace is drawn on. Drag on the pad, spin the wheel, right-click, and type in the box; the sheet reports whether Owlbear moved underneath, which you cannot see, because the sheet is covering it. Kept because its leak detector is the only way to re-check that a change has not started letting input through to Owlbear.
