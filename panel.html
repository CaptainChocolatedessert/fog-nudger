Connecting…

Everything that decides what gets traced is there: the map, the ink, the regions, and the button that stages them. It has to be its own surface because the answers are per-pixel verdicts, which only a picture can carry — and because the controls belong beside the thing they change rather than in a panel the picture would cover.

Staged proposals

Staged proposals are GM-only and produce no walls, so nothing here can affect play until you accept. Accepting moves them to the fog layer, where Dynamic Fog starts deriving walls. Going back to staging is the exact inverse and keeps every hand edit; re-tracing does not. Apply appearance restyles what is already staged from the workspace's fill and outline — an accepted shape is forced to full opacity regardless, because fog below that leaves a tint over ground the party has revealed.

Diagnostics

Trace, emit nothing runs the whole chain and writes the numbers to the log — coverage, region counts, and the area check, which is two independent computations of the same figure agreeing. The workspace draws the same partition; this is the only thing that reports it in numbers. What is here? is no longer a button: click the map in the workspace.

Workspace probe puts up an opaque full-screen sheet that keeps its own input, which is what the workspace is drawn on. Drag on the pad, spin the wheel, right-click, and type in the box; the sheet reports whether Owlbear moved underneath, which you cannot see, because the sheet is covering it. Kept because its leak detector is the only way to re-check that a change has not started letting input through to Owlbear.
